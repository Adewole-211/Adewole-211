# Hi, I’m Wole 👋

## About Me

I am a Logistics and Operations Analyst at Amazon with more than three years of experience supporting high-volume fulfilment-centre operations through data analysis, capacity planning, performance reporting and workflow automation.

I use SQL, Amazon Redshift, Python, R, Excel and Amazon QuickSight to validate operational data, investigate performance variance and translate complex datasets into practical actions. My work supports decisions involving backlog utilisation, staffing, throughput, network volume allocation, customer fulfilment and non-productive time.

I also deliver analytical and automation projects that improve reporting quality, operational visibility and stakeholder accountability.

## What I Do

- Monitor operational dashboards to identify capacity, staffing, backlog and fulfilment-flow risks.
- Compare planned, projected and actual performance to highlight emerging gaps.
- Build and maintain SQL datasets and Amazon QuickSight dashboards.
- Validate reporting outputs and investigate data-quality discrepancies.
- Automate recurring reports and stakeholder notifications using Python, R and APIs.
- Conduct root-cause analysis and translate findings into practical improvement actions.
- Present KPI trends and improvement opportunities during business reviews with senior operations leaders.
- Support and train new and returning team members on operational processes and analytical tools.

## Selected Project Experience

### Multi-KPI Operational Performance Dashboard

Developed and integrated multiple SQL queries in Amazon Redshift to create a dashboard-ready dataset covering operational performance, capacity constraints and other key fulfilment-centre metrics. Built the final dashboard in Amazon QuickSight to support performance monitoring and adherence to network standards.

### Automated Project Governance Reporting

Built a scheduled Python workflow that extracts project data from Asana through an API, processes it using pandas and identifies outstanding and late-completed tasks. The workflow runs automatically on a virtual machine and distributes weekly Outlook reports to project stakeholders.

### Fulfilment-Centre Closure Alerts

Manage an automated Asana-to-Slack workflow that sends advance notifications of scheduled fulfilment-centre closures to relevant operational channels. Responsibilities include maintaining source tasks, monitoring the workflow and managing API authentication.

### Business Review Automation

Created an R workflow that transforms recurring KPI data into automated visualisations for monthly business reviews. The reporting enables senior operations leaders to track performance, understand variance, investigate root causes and prioritise improvement opportunities.

### Operational Rate Performance Analysis

Used Python and pandas to compare predicted, planned and actual operational rates across process groups, shifts and time intervals. Produced Plotly visualisations to highlight performance variance and support operational analysis.
